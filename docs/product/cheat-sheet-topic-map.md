# Canonical curriculum: IFR Cheat Sheet V7.1

Mapped 13 September 2026 from the supplied Ben Montgomery-Schinkel IFR Cheat Sheet, dated 30 March 2024. Page numbers are the printed PDF pages (also the PDF viewer page numbers). Luna extracted the contents and checked heading starts in page text; the owner reviewed the hierarchy and migration decisions.

**Status: source inventory, not regulatory sign-off.** These are source topics to author, not approved existing lessons. Candidate IDs identify material to compare, never permission to copy it. Official references, currentness and teaching objectives must be completed in each authored lesson. Adjacent topics can share a page; a range does not imply ownership of every statement on those pages.

The source's chapter headings are navigation groups, not extra overview lessons. In particular, Alternate Requirements is a parent group for its named subtopics. This avoids recreating a summary plus duplicate detail lessons.

## Migration decisions

- Every CS-* summary is a comparison candidate; the CS library does not remain a second syllabus.
- MSC-* duplicates named General Operational Knowledge topics; keep one canonical topic.
- QF-*, TRAP-*, AIR-* and ADV-* are deferred collections. A useful question may later be rewritten inside its parent lesson after source review; its presence in an old bank is not sufficient justification.
- IFR-* extensions require explicit mapping and evidence before inclusion. Do not create catch-all administrative or airline sections for them.
- Nothing in this map authorises deletion of user progress, automatic remapping of completion states, or bulk import of old content. Old completion means the old module was read, not that its replacement has been studied.
