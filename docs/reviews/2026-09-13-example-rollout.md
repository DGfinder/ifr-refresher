## Source and editorial review

Root checked the 9 July 2026 [Part 91 MOS](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2020L01514/2026-07-09/2026-07-09/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html): 8.05/8.07/8.08, 10.03–10.06, 11.02, 14.03, 15.06 and 24.02. Existing equipment/minima source checks are recorded in the pilot review. Minima retains its explicit 2024/current distinction. [CASA instrument approach guidance](https://www.casa.gov.au/licences-and-certificates/pilots/ratings-reviews-and-endorsements/instrument-ratings/instrument-approach-operations) supports the ILS missed-approach example. Access date: 13 September 2026.

Gradient values are dimensional arithmetic using 1 NM = 1,852 m and 1 ft = 0.3048 m: 3% gives approximately 182.28 ft/NM, then 364.57 ft/min at 120 kt and 455.71 at 150 kt. Rounded values are 365 and 456. They are not asserted chart or aircraft performance data.

Root rejected duplicate missed-approach drafts, an ambiguous RNP/DA example, an unsupported Adelaide tower closure, suggestions to increase approach minima without a specific method, and automation-specific holding advice. Replaced third-person drafting with second-person prose. Recency exemptions, full communications-failure procedures, holding geometry and exact navigation tolerances were not expanded from partially checked drafts. Existing sample briefings already illustrate their topics. These omissions avoid forcing weak examples onto every page; they are not claims those topics cannot be improved later.
