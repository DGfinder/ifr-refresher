```text
VHF Theoretical Maximum Range 
To determine the theoretical maximum range of VHF transmissions, the following 
formula can be used: 
d = 1.23 (√(Htx) + √(Hrx)) 
Where: 
d = distance in NM 
Htx = elevation in feet of transmitter 
Hrx = height in feet above ground level of receiver 
  
```
